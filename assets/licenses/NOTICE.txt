TOOT TUBES

Original project code, procedural ring/rider/course artwork, four-rider SVG icon,
and synthesized sound effects/music were created for this project.
No downloaded models, textures, recordings or commercial game media are bundled.

Original audio assets (generated by tools/generate_audio.py): thrust_attack,
thrust_loop, thrust_release, impact, fall, pickup, ui, checkpoint, win, timeout,
and sky_theme. The 16-beat melody is an original procedural composition.

Fredoka font: The Fredoka Project Authors. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
The complete font copyright and license are bundled at assets/fonts/OFL.txt.

Godot Engine: Godot Engine contributors, MIT license.
The engine's complete license and third-party notices are available in the
in-game Credits > Read licenses view, generated from the engine's own notices.

GUT is a development-only MIT test dependency, excluded from game exports.
